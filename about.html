Our Approach

A digital solutions partner, not just a vendor.

At Tech Miss3, we believe technology should actively eliminate friction, not introduce more complexity. We began by closely studying therapy centre workflows, identifying how therapists, child development specialists, and clinic directors struggle with disjointed paper registers, double-bookings, and lost milestone journals.

That workflow insight led to our flagship software, PointScheduler. We bring that practical, workflow-first engineering mindset to every client project: from AI-driven operational workflows to high-performance web applications and native mobile apps.
