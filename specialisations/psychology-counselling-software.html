How It Works

Clinical Psychology in PointScheduler

  1. 1

    Your Team. Ready.

    Add each therapist with their specialisations and working hours.

  2. 2

    Register the Child.

    Register the child with Clinical Psychology, their fee, and wallet or package.

  3. 3

    No Double Bookings.

    Book single or repeating sessions. Clashes are stopped before saving.

  4. 4

    Session Done. Details Sorted.

    Start and complete each session, then add notes, activities and photos.

  5. 5

    One Child. One Clear Plan.

    Set goals in the child's lesson plan and track each goal's status.

  6. 6

    Bills, Made Simple.

    Charge completed sessions to the wallet or package and print monthly bills.

Case Study

Sara's Month

A monthly package of weekly sessions.

  1. 1

    The admin registers Sara with Clinical Psychology and picks Package. In Package Setup, Clinical Psychology is set at ₹4,000 a month for 4 sessions. The payment is recorded in Package Payments.

  2. 2

    The admin books 1-hour sessions every Saturday at 10 am.

  3. 3

    After each session, the psychologist taps Mark Complete and writes the session summary.

  4. 4

    One Saturday is cancelled, and no package session is used.

Questions centres ask

Can a child have Clinical Psychology alongside other therapies? expand_more

Yes. Add every therapy the child needs, with one fee for all or a fee for each.

Can Clinical Psychology sessions repeat? expand_more

Yes. Repeat them daily, weekly on chosen days, or monthly, until a date or a set number of sessions.

What happens if a session is cancelled? expand_more

Each child can have free cancellations every month. After that your cancellation fee applies. A package session is only used when a session is completed.
