Healthcare Data Governance

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective for all PointScheduler platforms and services

1. Introduction & Commitment to Clinical Privacy

At Tech Miss3, we recognize the critical importance of confidentiality and data security in healthcare and pediatric therapy operations. This Privacy Policy explains how PointScheduler collects, processes, stores, and protects data when Child Development Centres (CDCs), therapy clinics, healthcare providers, therapists, and guardians interact with our platform.

PointScheduler serves as a specialized business and clinical practice management platform. All patient, clinical, and financial records processed through PointScheduler remain the exclusive property of the subscribing clinic or organization.

2. Categories of Information Processed

  • Clinic & Administrative Data: Organization name, clinic location, administrator credentials, billing preferences, authorized clinic signature, and clinic branding.
  • Practitioner & Staff Data: Clinician names, specialties (e.g., OT, Speech, ABA, PT), professional contact numbers, schedules, attendance registers, and leave records.
  • Patient & Guardian Records: Patient identification, contact details, appointment occurrences, therapy notes, developmental milestone records, and treatment package balances.
  • Financial & Billing Data: Service fees, invoice records, payment reconciliation logs, and wallet transaction balances. PointScheduler does not store raw credit/debit card details.
  • Technical Metadata: Authenticated session tokens, IP addresses, device identifiers, and system audit logs required for security monitoring.

3. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Multi-Tenant Isolation

PointScheduler is architected around strict tenant and role boundaries:

  • Center Isolation: Clinical and financial records for one centre are strictly isolated from all other centres, preventing unauthorized cross-tenant data access.
  • Role Permissions: Therapists can only access patients and sessions explicitly scheduled with them. Receptionists handle attendance and booking without accessing private clinical journals. Accountants access billing reports without modifying clinical protocols.
  • Guardian Privacy: Through the upcoming Parent App, guardians authenticate independently and can only view their own child's progress summaries, schedules, and receipts.

4. Security Standards & Data Transmission

We deploy robust, modern security safeguards:

  • All communication across web portals and mobile applications is encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.3/HTTPS.
  • Database connections utilize secure pooling with encrypted channels and automated snapshot backups.
  • Authentication utilizes JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with time-bounded expiration and cryptographic signature validation.

5. Data Sharing & Non-Commercialization

We do NOT sell, lease, or monetize clinic, patient, or child data. Data is never shared with advertisers or third-party brokers. Data is processed solely through vetted infrastructure service providers (cloud hosting, notification delivery, and database engines) strictly necessary to run the PointScheduler service.

6. Data Ownership, Portability & Retention

Subscribing clinics retain full ownership of all patient, financial, and clinical records. Upon request or subscription conclusion, clinics may request a comprehensive export of their records in standard spreadsheet and database formats.
