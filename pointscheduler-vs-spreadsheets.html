PointScheduler vs Spreadsheets and paper registers

Excel and a register book got your centre this far. Here's an honest look at where they start costing you time, and what PointScheduler does instead.

Side by side

The same jobs, done two ways

JobExcel and registersPointScheduler
Booking a term of sessionsCopy rows week by weekOne recurring series, up to 365 sessions
Avoiding clashesSpotted when two families arriveTherapist and child double-booking blocked; working hours checked
AttendanceTicks in a registerCompleted, Cancelled or No-show marked in the app
Cancellation rulesRemembered by the front deskFree cancellations per month, then the fee, applied every time
Family balancesA sheet per familyPrepaid wallet deducted per completed session
Low balancesFound at billing timeAlert below ₹500, adjustable per child
Monthly billsWorked out by handGenerated from completed sessions in a click
PackagesTally marksSessions used against each specialisation's limit
Staff attendance and salaryAnother register and a calculatorQR, location or face check-in; salary generated monthly
Expenses and reimbursementsReceipts in a drawerBill photo, who paid, reimbursement status
Parent updatesPhone callsWhatsApp templates from your number (Growth and Enterprise)
More than one branchA file per branchOne organisation with multi-branch reports
Who sees whatAnyone with the fileRole-based access and custom roles

A spreadsheet is fine for one therapist with a handful of children. Once you have several therapists, packages or a second branch, the hours spent reconciling start to add up.
