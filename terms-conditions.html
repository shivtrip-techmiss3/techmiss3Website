2. Description of Platform & Services

PointScheduler provides healthcare scheduling, multi-specialty session management (Occupational Therapy, Speech Therapy, Special Education, Applied Behavior Analysis, Physiotherapy), room allocation, standardized billing, SOAP therapy notes, and role-based operational oversight for pediatric clinics and rehabilitation centres.

3. Trial Subscriptions & Commercial Terms

3.1 Introductory Trial: PointScheduler offers an introductory evaluation trial of 15 days at ₹99. The trial allows access to scheduling and management features to evaluate clinical workflow fit.

3.2 Commercial Subscriptions: Ongoing access is provided via monthly or annual subscription agreements. Invoices are issued with GST compliance as applicable under Indian commercial regulations.

3.3 Cancellation: Clinics may cancel subscriptions at the end of their active billing term with no punitive exit fees. Data export assistance will be provided as outlined in Section 6.

4. Clinic Responsibilities & Role-Based Access

Subscribing clinics are responsible for:

  • Assigning appropriate Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) tiers to their clinicians, therapists, and front-desk personnel.
  • Maintaining confidentiality of credentials and two-factor authentication tokens.
  • Ensuring necessary patient or guardian consent is obtained for session documentation and pediatric clinical records.

5. Clinical Practice & Medical Disclaimer

PointScheduler is an administrative, operations, scheduling, and billing management software platform. It is not an automated medical diagnostic tool or emergency dispatch system. Medical, therapeutic, and diagnostic decisions remain exclusively the responsibility of certified practitioners and clinic leadership.
