Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers for healthcare directors, clinicians, and practice operations managers.

What happens after the ₹99 trial? expand_more

You can continue seamlessly on our monthly or annual CDC management plan with full data retention, or cancel anytime with zero lock-in. Your therapists, patients, and session notes remain securely stored.

Can I import my existing clinic data? expand_more

Yes! Our solutions team assists with zero-downtime data migration from Excel, spreadsheets, or other scheduling software directly into PointScheduler so you don't lose past appointments, patient files, or balance ledgers.

Is patient therapy data safe and private? expand_more

Absolutely. PointScheduler enforces strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Therapists only see their assigned patients, while front-desk and clinical directors have strictly segregated permissions. All medical records and financial logs are fully encrypted and backed up daily.

What is PointScheduler? expand_more

PointScheduler is specialized therapy centre management software created by Tech Miss3. It centralizes practitioner rosters, patient records, recurring therapy schedules, attendance check-ins, clinical notes, treatment packages, and overall clinic performance into one integrated web and mobile platform.

Who is PointScheduler designed for? expand_more

It is purposefully designed for therapy-intensive clinical environments: Child Development Centres (CDCs), Autism/ABA clinics, Speech-Language pathology practices, Occupational Therapy suites, Paediatric and Adult Physiotherapy centres, and Multi-specialty Rehabilitation facilities.

Can PointScheduler manage complex recurring therapy appointments? expand_more

Yes. Multi-week and multi-month therapy blocks are fundamental to its architecture. The system checks therapist availability, room reservations, and equipment buffers across all target cycles, preventing double-bookings.

How does PointScheduler prevent room and sensory gym collisions? expand_more

PointScheduler links therapist scheduling directly with physical room inventory. When an appointment is scheduled, it validates both room availability (such as specialized sensory gyms or quiet speech booths) and clinician rosters simultaneously, helping prevent room collisions and scheduling conflicts.

Is PointScheduler part of Tech Miss3? expand_more

Yes. PointScheduler is built and supported by Tech Miss3, an Udyam-registered healthcare technology and IT solutions company. You can reach the same team for ongoing support, custom features, or broader software solutions.
