Clear Clinical Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers for healthcare directors, clinicians, and practice operations managers.

How do I start using PointScheduler? expand_more

Sign up with your centre's details or book a free demo. We set up your centre, therapists and specialisations, and you run your centre on PointScheduler for a 15-day trial. After the trial, continue on a monthly or annual plan with all your data kept. See the pricing page for details.

Can I import my existing clinic data? expand_more

Yes! Our solutions team assists with zero-downtime data migration from Excel, spreadsheets, or other scheduling software directly into PointScheduler so you don't lose past appointments, patient files, or balance ledgers.

Is patient therapy data safe and private? expand_more

Yes. Every record is tied to your centre and kept apart from other centres. Each team member sees what their role allows, passwords are never stored as plain text, and your data is backed up. More on data protection.

What is PointScheduler? expand_more

PointScheduler is child therapy centre software built by Tech Miss3. It runs appointments, monthly billing, goals and progress, therapist HR, centre expenses and prepaid wallets with top-ups for CDCs and therapy centres, on web and mobile.

Who is PointScheduler designed for? expand_more

Child Development Centres (CDCs) and therapy centres for occupational therapy, speech therapy, autism and ABA, special education and physiotherapy, including centres that offer several of these under one roof.

Can PointScheduler manage complex recurring therapy appointments? expand_more

Yes. Book sessions once and repeat them daily, weekly or monthly. PointScheduler blocks a therapist or a child being booked twice at the same time and blocks times outside a therapist's working hours.

How does PointScheduler prevent double booking? expand_more

PointScheduler blocks a therapist or a child being booked twice at the same time and blocks times outside a therapist's working hours.

Is PointScheduler part of Tech Miss3? expand_more

Yes. PointScheduler is built and supported by Tech Miss3, a Bengaluru-based, Udyam-registered HealthTech company. The same team handles your onboarding, support and new features.
