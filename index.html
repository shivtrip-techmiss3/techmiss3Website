Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to the questions we hear most from new clients.

What services does Tech Miss3 offer? expand_more

Tech Miss3 offers three core services: AI-based business automation, custom website development, and mobile app development for Android and iOS. We also help integrate these solutions with the tools you already use.

How long does it take to build a website or app? expand_more

Timelines depend on the scope and features you need. After an initial consultation, we share a clear project schedule so you know exactly what to expect before any work begins.

Is Tech Miss3 a registered business? expand_more

Yes, Tech Miss3 is a Udyam-registered MSME, so you’re working with a formally recognized business partner.

Do you offer support after the website or app goes live? expand_more

Yes. We offer post-launch support and maintenance plans so your website, app, or automation keeps running smoothly as your business grows.

What is PointScheduler and how do I use it to book a slot? expand_more

PointScheduler is Tech Miss3’s built-in booking tool. Pick an available time in the booking section below, share a few details about your project, and you’ll get an instant, time-zone aware confirmation with no back-and-forth emails.

How much does a project cost? expand_more

Pricing depends on the scope, features, and platforms involved. Share your requirements through the contact form and we’ll get back to you with a free, no-obligation quote.
