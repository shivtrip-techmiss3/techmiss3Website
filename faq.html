What happens after the ₹99 trial? expand_more

You can continue seamlessly on our monthly or annual CDC management plan with full data retention, or cancel anytime with zero lock-in. Your therapists, patients, and session notes remain securely stored.

Can I import my existing clinic data? expand_more

Yes! Our solutions team assists with zero-downtime data migration from Excel, spreadsheets, or other scheduling software directly into PointScheduler so you don't lose past appointments, patient files, or balance ledgers.

Is patient therapy data safe and private? expand_more

Absolutely. PointScheduler enforces strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Therapists only see their assigned patients, while front-desk and clinical directors have strictly segregated permissions. All medical records and financial logs are fully encrypted and backed up daily.

What is TechMiss3? expand_more

TechMiss3 is an Udyam-registered healthcare technology and digital software company based in India. As the creators of PointScheduler, we build specialized clinic management tools for Child Development Centres (CDCs) and therapy practices, alongside modern web platforms, mobile apps, and AI business solutions.

Where is TechMiss3 registered? expand_more

TechMiss3 is a formally registered MSME under the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, Government of India (Udyam Registration). All client engagements feature transparent agreements and verifiable deliverables.

What is PointScheduler? expand_more

PointScheduler is a specialized therapy centre management platform created by TechMiss3. It replaces physical registers and generic spreadsheets by unifying practitioner rosters, patient milestones, recurring session bookings, room allocations, post-session SOAP notes, and package reconciliation into one integrated cloud system.

Who can use PointScheduler? expand_more

PointScheduler is built specifically for therapy-intensive practices: Child Development Centres (CDCs), Autism & ABA practices, Speech-Language Pathology suites, Paediatric Occupational Therapy clinics, and Multi-specialty Rehabilitation centres.

How does PointScheduler manage recurring therapy appointments without collisions? expand_more

PointScheduler links therapist availability directly with specialized room inventory (e.g. sensory gyms or sound-proof speech booths). When booking multi-week or multi-month therapy blocks, the system validates both room and clinician schedules simultaneously, preventing double-bookings.

Is there a mobile app for parents? expand_more

Yes, the PointScheduler Parent App is currently in development (marked as Coming Soon). It will give guardians direct visibility into upcoming therapy sessions, milestone growth charts, home-practice exercises from therapists, and package balances.

How can clinics get early access to the Parent App? expand_more

Clinics adopting PointScheduler can register their interest for early beta pilot participation. When the mobile portal launches, participating practices will receive guardian onboarding guidance and mobile collateral.

Is clinic and patient data secure? expand_more

Yes. PointScheduler uses encrypted connections and granular role-based permissions (so clinicians only view their assigned patient journals) to safeguard clinical records.

Does the clinic retain ownership of its data? expand_more

Absolutely. You retain 100% legal ownership of your patient records, therapist rosters, and attendance histories. Clinic exports can be generated whenever needed.

How does package and session reconciliation work? expand_more

When a child completes an OT, speech, or psychology appointment, the attendance check-in automatically deducts one credit from their active prepaid package. Clinic staff can instantly see remaining balance and renewals.

Do you provide training and onboarding support? expand_more

Yes. Our solutions specialist walks your administrative staff and clinical practitioners through setting up rooms, import rosters, configuring milestone goals, and handling everyday check-ins.

How do we book a live walkthrough? expand_more

You can instantly select a 30-minute demonstration on our live Google Calendar scheduler at calendar.app.google/... or contact us directly via email at contact@techmiss3.com.
