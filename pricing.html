Pricing questions

How much does the trial cost? expand_more

The trial is ₹99 for 15 days. It includes every feature and specialisation, setup of your centre, an onboarding call and WhatsApp support.

What happens after the 15 days? expand_more

Choose a Solo, Growth or Enterprise plan, monthly or annual, and carry on with all your data.

Which plans are there? expand_more

Solo is for a single therapist running their own practice. Growth is for centres with a growing team of therapists and staff. Enterprise is for larger teams and organisations with several branches. Message us on WhatsApp for a quote.

Can I import my existing clinic data? expand_more

Yes. Our team helps you move appointments, patient files and balances from Excel, registers or other software into PointScheduler.

What about refunds? expand_more

Trial and plan payments are covered by our refund and cancellation policy.
